# 📊 Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze and monitor key supply chain performance metrics.

The dashboard transforms raw supply chain data into meaningful insights through interactive KPIs, charts, filters, and visual analysis.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing different aspects of supply chain operations, including:

* Revenue performance
* Product sales
* Production volumes
* Shipping and lead times
* Transportation modes
* Product categories
* Inventory / stock levels
* Inspection results
* Geographic performance

The dashboard provides an interactive environment for exploring supply chain performance and supporting **data-driven decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Monitor overall supply chain performance.
* Analyze revenue generated across different locations and product types.
* Compare product sales and production volumes.
* Analyze shipping and lead times.
* Understand the distribution of SKUs across transportation modes.
* Evaluate inspection results for different product categories.
* Identify patterns and differences between locations and products.
* Present complex data in a simple and interactive format.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Revenue & Product Sales by Location

Analyzes the relationship between:

* Revenue generated
* Number of products sold
* Different locations

This helps identify locations with stronger revenue and sales performance.

### 2. Transportation Mode Analysis

A breakdown of SKU counts across different transportation modes:

* Road
* Rail
* Air
* Sea

This provides an overview of how products are distributed across transportation methods.

### 3. Revenue by Product Type

Shows the contribution of each product category to total revenue.

Product categories include:

* Skincare
* Haircare
* Cosmetics

### 4. Production Volume by Product Type

Compares production volumes across different product categories to understand production distribution.

### 5. Shipping Time Analysis

Analyzes shipping times across product types and helps compare logistics performance between categories.

### 6. SKU & Inspection Results

Displays SKU counts according to inspection results:

* Fail
* Pass
* Pending

This helps monitor product quality and identify categories with higher inspection issues.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters that allow users to explore the data dynamically.

Available filters include:

* **Location**
* **Product Type**
* **Routes**

Users can select different values to dynamically update the KPIs and visualizations.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

Some of the main insights that can be explored through the dashboard include:

* **Mumbai and Kolkata** show strong revenue performance compared with the other displayed locations.
* **Skincare** represents the largest share of revenue among the displayed product categories.
* Transportation activity is distributed across **Road, Rail, Air, and Sea**.
* Shipping times vary between product categories.
* Inspection results provide visibility into products that passed, failed, or are still pending inspection.
* Product categories differ in both revenue contribution and production volume.

> These insights are based on the dashboard's current data and selected filters.
